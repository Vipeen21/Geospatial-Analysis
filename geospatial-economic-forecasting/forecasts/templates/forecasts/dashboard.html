Where To Find Open Input Data

  • NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index): use NASA Earthdata vegetation index products such as MODIS MOD13Q1, or use Copernicus Sentinel data and calculate NDVI as area average.
  • Nightlight intensity: use NASA Earthdata Nighttime Lights or NASA Black Marble/VIIRS products and calculate area average radiance.
  • Capital formation proxy: use state-level tables from the RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States or national capital formation tables from MoSPI. For district-level work, use the closest available official proxy consistently.
